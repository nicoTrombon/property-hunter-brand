Personal project

Barcelona Property Hunter is a personal tool for organizing apartment alerts and preparing a private shortlist. This website provides project information only. There are no public accounts, subscriptions, payments or brokerage services.

Listing accuracy

Listings originate from third-party alerts. Information may be incomplete, outdated, duplicated or parsed incorrectly. Prices, availability, property characteristics and bathroom counts require independent verification with the advertiser. A ranking is a configurable heuristic, not a valuation, endorsement or recommendation to buy.

Financial illustrations

Any mortgage, tax, cost or cash estimates in private reports are illustrative calculations based on assumptions. They are not professional financial, tax, legal or lending advice, and are not financing offers or approvals.

Third-party services

The project is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google, GitHub or Idealista. Those services and property advertisers have their own terms. The tool uses email alerts authorized by the inbox owner and does not provide authorization to access another person's mailbox or restricted property data.

Availability

The application is experimental and may change, stop or miss alerts. It provides no guarantee of complete discovery, uninterrupted operation, successful purchases or accurate rankings.
